为什么我选择 LLM Post-Training 作为博士方向 Why I Chose LLM Post-Training for PhD

从 BCI 到 LLM 的迁移，看似跨度很大，实则共享同一个核心问题：如何让模型在动态环境中持续适应而不遗忘。 BCI 里叫跨被试迁移（Cross-Subject Transfer），LLM 里叫持续学习（Continual Learning）和测试时适应（Test-Time Adaptation）。 两者的本质都是：在数据分布不断变化的情况下，用极少的标注成本保持模型性能。 The jump from BCI to LLM looks huge, but both share the same core: how to continually adapt without forgetting in dynamic environments. In BCI it's cross-subject transfer; in LLM it's continual learning and test-time adaptation. The essence is the same: maintain performance with minimal labeled data under distribution shift.

这个洞察来自我在做 NeuroLoop 时的挫败感：我花了大量时间调 EEG 信号，却发现瓶颈不在信号处理，而在"如何让模型快速适应新用户"。 这正是 LLM Post-Training 社区正在攻克的命题。 This insight came from the frustration during NeuroLoop: I spent months tuning EEG signals, only to realize the bottleneck wasn't signal processing, but "how to make the model adapt to new users quickly" — exactly what the LLM Post-Training community is tackling.

电梯 SaaS 创业 30 天：技术决策复盘 Elevator SaaS 30-Day Sprint: Technical Decisions

第一周决定用 Clean Architecture + DDD，现在回头看，对于一个人开发的 MVP 来说有些过度设计。 但好处是：当我需要把离线队列模块拆成独立服务时，边界非常清晰。 Event Sourcing 也一度让我陷入"为了审计而审计"的陷阱，后来简化为只记录关键业务事件（TaskCreated, PhotoUploaded）。 Week 1: chose Clean Architecture + DDD. In hindsight, overkill for a solo MVP. But the benefit showed when I needed to extract the offline queue into a standalone service — boundaries were already clear. Event Sourcing also trapped me in "audit for audit's sake"; later simplified to only key events (TaskCreated, PhotoUploaded).
